I've been a U.S. diplomat since 2010, with assignments in Europe, Africa, and Central America. Before that, I trained as a robotics engineer. When I can't find a fix for a problem, I like building one myself.

Things I've built

  • FEHB Survey 2026

    2026

    FSBP premiums are going up a lot in 2027, and I was trying to decide whether to switch plans. There's no shortage of opinions in the Foreign Service Facebook groups about which plans work overseas, but there was no way to compare them. So I made an anonymous survey and shared it in those groups. Eighty households responded, and I posted what they said.

  • Global city light analysis

    2025 · GitHub

    At work we needed to compare economic activity between cities in a lot of different countries, and many of them don't have reliable local GDP data. I needed something I could measure the same way everywhere, so I wrote a script that uses satellite images of nighttime lights as a stand-in.

  • Color-changing lamp for my son

    2021 · Hackaday

    When my son was born, I wanted a soft lamp that would change color when he played with it and that I could control from Home Assistant. I couldn't find anything I liked, so I bought a Mr. Miffy lamp and built my own light module for it. Most of the time went into learning (read: fumbling through) Zigbee.
